# Published regression data

[English](fixtures.md) | [Français](fixtures.fr.md)

The test data is fictional/synthetic. It exists to expose concrete regressions,
not to demonstrate model quality on a real meeting or represent actual people.

- `src-core/tests/fixtures/`: synthetic Orion/Atlas scenarios, topic candidates
  and a separate oracle/erratum. Explicit synthetic declarations and the source
  contracts accompany the original fixtures.
- `ui/tests/fixtures/`: snapshots generated by Rust producer examples, consumed
  by the frontend and by cross-language contract tests.
- `docs/evaluations/orion-2026-09-28/`: four approved model-result states used by
  `src-core/tests/verified_report.rs`. These are fixed regression inputs from
  the fictional Orion scenario, not a publication of private meeting data.
- Audio envelope fixtures are generated at verification time by Rust into a
  temporary directory. They are not user recordings and are not checked in.

Keep the declared scenario/oracle distinction and exact paths. A test oracle is
not a human confirmation of model output. Do not invent a fixture to disguise a
failing transport or remove a regression because a source export is incomplete.
New fixtures must be synthetic, minimal, documented and redistributable under
the appropriate licence. No real media or transcript may be attached to issues.
